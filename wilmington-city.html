About

Wilmington is an urban port city in the U.S. state of North Carolina. It was incorporated in 1739. It is located in the southeastern region of North Carolina. It is the eighth-most populous city in the state. The county seat of New Hanover County, it is the principal city of the Wilmington metropolitan area[2].

The Wilmington metropolitan area includes New Hanover, Brunswick, and Pender counties[6].

Population

As of the 2020 census, there were 115,451 people living in the city[7].

Average Income

As of the 2020 census, the median household income was $63,165[7]. North Carolina's average income is $73,958[8]. Compared to the rest of the state, Wilmington has a lower average income.
