About

Charlotte is the urban metropolitan area of the U.S. state of North Carolina. It was incorporated in 1768. It is located in the south-central region of North Carolina. It is the most populous city in the state, largest metropolitan area in the Caroinas, and the fourth largest in the Southeastern United States[5].

The largest metropolitan area in the Southeastern US is Jacksonville, Florida[8].

Population

As of the 2020 census, there were 874,579 people living in the city[7].

Average Income

As of the 2020 census, the median household income was $86,416[7]. North Carolina's average income is $73,958[6]. Compared to the rest of the state, Charlotte has a higher average income.
