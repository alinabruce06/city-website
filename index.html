About

North Carolina is a state in the Southeastern region of the United States. At the 2020 census, the state had a population of 10,439,388[1]. It is bordered by Virginia to the north, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, South Carolina to the south, Georgia to the southwest, and Tennessee to the west. The state is the 28th-largest and ninth-most populous of the United States. North Carolina was inhabited by Carolina Algonquian, Iroquoian, and Siouan speaking tribes of Native Americans prior to the arrival of Europeans[2].

Over four hundred years ago, more than thirty Native American tribes were living in present-day North Carolina[3].

Raleigh, NC ★

Raleigh is the state's capital[2].

Charlotte, NC

Charlotte is the most populous of the state's cities and one of the fastest growing cities in the United States[2].

Wilmington, NC

Wilmington is a port city in New Hanover County, North Carolina, United States[4].
