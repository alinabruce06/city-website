About

Raleigh is the urban captial city of the U.S. state of North Carolina. It was incorporated in 1770. It is located in the northeast central region of North Carolina. It is the second-most populous city in the state, tenth most populous city in the Southeast, the largest city in the Research Triangle area, and the 39th most populous city in the U.S. Known as the "City of Oaks" for its oak-lined streets. It is the county seat of Wake County and named after Sir Walter Raleigh (Raleigh, North Carolina 2025).

Sir Walter Raleigh founded the lost Roanoke Colony[6].

Population

As of the 2020 census, there were 467,665 people living in the city[7].

Average Income

As of the 2020 census, the median household income was $85,060[7]. North Carolina's average income is $73,958[8]. Compared to the rest of the state, Raleigh has a higher average income.

Weather

Raleigh has a four-season humid subtropical climate. Winters are cold, with an average temperature of 41.9 °F. The average annual rainfall is 46.07 in. February is the diest month. Summers are hot with an average temperature of 80.5 °F (Weather averages Raleigh, North Carolina 2025).
